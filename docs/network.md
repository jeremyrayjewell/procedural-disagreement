# The network, 3:00.0–3:30.0

The start of the 75-second instrumental climax. The stations become one system; then the
system turns out to be organised around something that is not there.

Nothing after 3:30.0 is authored. This document states what is true at 3:30.0.

The code is in `procedural_disagreement/core/network.py`. The scene layer adds nothing new of
its own: it moves stations and pieces of the level that already exist.

## 3:00–3:20: one system

**Falling into step.** At 3:00 each of the 31 stations runs the same three-bar procedure on its
own time, in seventeen different phases. Around the junction of the heavy trunk and the heavy
main they now fall into step, in rings 40 m apart:

- each station's procedure slips, never backward, until it stands where its ring stands;
- the ring at the junction starts at 3:00.0 and each ring further out 1.25 s later; a station
  takes 8 s to lock, so the inner ring is in step by 3:08 and the outermost by 3:11.75;
- every ring ends on a bar line, and neighbouring rings stand exactly one bar apart.

Because the procedure is three bars long, the result is three phases a bar apart: when one ring
turns its heads to its platforms, the next is running its carriages and the next is lowering
its yokes. What one ring finishes the next begins.

**Stations taking part:** 40. The refrain's 31, and 9 further east that are built out only from
3:00 (they were plain units before, as the locked film shows them, and come out of the dark
over six seconds).

## 3:20–3:30: the lane

**What it is.** A straight diagonal gap through the stations: it starts at (121.0, 187.8) m,
just south of the heavy main and east of the heavy trunk, and runs 60 m at 35° south of east.
For 8 m either side of its axis no station, bay or spur stands. It was not built. It is the
widest straight gap among the stations ahead of where the refrain ends, found by search; the
stations were placed long before, by the chutes below them.

Nothing marks it. No line, light, outline or object is ever placed in it.

**It is finite, and that is part of it.** On the same axis the first station comes within the
lane's width at 69.6 m. The lane cannot be made longer without moving a station, and no station
is moved. So the passage has two parts: the network makes and keeps an empty corridor (3:20 to
about 3:27), and then the camera comes to where the system's accommodation of absence stops
(about 3:27 to 3:30).

**How it becomes legible.** From 3:20.0:

1. **Every procedure stops reaching its end.** Over 2.5 s, yokes come down only 40% as far,
   carriages run 55% as far and no longer extend.
2. **Fifteen stations beside the lane leave their procedures and hold.** Each, nearest first,
   takes 3 s: its head turns from its own empty platform to face the nearest point of the lane;
   its yoke stays 0.3 m down; its carriage stays 0.8 m along its rail. Suspended, not reset.
3. **The lane is cleared ahead of the camera.** Everything inside its width (21 existing
   pieces: 13 cells, 4 trunk runs, 3 fine runs and one 24 m length of the heavy main) goes down
   by its own height and 6 cm more, so the ground closes over it and no stub is left. They go
   in 5 m bands along the lane, on the beat grid: the first two bands at 3:20.0, the next three
   a beat apart, the rest a beat and a half apart, the last starting at 3:28.4. The length of
   heavy main at the lane's mouth is the largest thing to go (1.66 m, at 3:20.0). From 12 m
   into the lane on, every piece is down at least half a second before the camera reaches it.
4. **Eight overhead frames turn.** The four stations nearest the lane on each side (195, 178,
   177, 211, 194, 210, 209, 225, in that order from 3:20.6) turn their whole overhead frame,
   posts, beam and yoke together, 12° about the platform, all in the same sense: a third of the
   35° that would square a frame to the lane. Never two at once. No part of a turned frame
   comes nearer than 9.6 m to the axis.
5. **Nothing enters it.** A test asserts that nothing within the lane's width ever rises.

**The camera** is on the lane's axis from 3:20.0 and travels down it at 5.5 m/s, 6.25 m above
the level. It comes in looking 10° down and goes on looking down while the lane lasts (9.6° to
10° from 3:20 to 3:27), which keeps the lane's floor in the picture. From 3:27 it lifts its
gaze, to 5° at 3:30 and still lifting: what is beyond the lane comes back into view, and is
meant to. Its minimum clearance from any structure in the whole passage is 5.4 m.

**What closes the lane.** Two stations, either side of the axis: 224 at (181.1, 135.9) m, 79 m
along and 8.0 m to the right, a plain unit; and 237 at (197.3, 142.7) m, 88 m along and 6.8 m
to the left, built out with machinery. At 3:30.0 they are 28 m and 37 m from the camera and
stand either side of the centre of the frame. Neither is moved or animated differently here.
They are the obvious place for whatever the network does next.

**Rhyme with 1:20.** At 1:20 one room organised itself around a point where a limb was not,
while the camera held still; at 1:30 the body recruited a piece of the room. At 3:20 a network
organises itself around a lane where nothing is, while the camera moves through it; at 3:30 the
lane ends.
